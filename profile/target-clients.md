- EU-based clients with English as the main working language
- Remote-first or hybrid engagements with minimal travel
- Contract durations of 6+ months preferred, with flexibility for shorter high-impact work

- Food and consumer packaged goods manufacturing
- Medtech and regulated manufacturing environments
- Automotive and discrete manufacturing
- Other mid-to-large manufacturers needing structured planning and change processes

- Supply chain and operations leaders needing SAP IBP and S/4HANA alignment
- IT / SAP leads responsible for planning, PP, and integration landscapes

- SAP IBP needs to be implemented or stabilised alongside S/4HANA PP
- IBP–S/4HANA integration via CI-DS is critical to project success
- Change management and compliance workflows are part of the scope
- There is a need for structured training and enablement of internal teams
