# Consulting Services

## 1. SAP IBP Planning Design and Rollout

- Design and implement SAP IBP S&OP, demand, and supply planning processes
- Align IBP design with existing or planned S/4HANA PP and logistics processes
- Define data flows, planning calendars, and integration points to support realistic planning cycles

Outputs:
- IBP process design documents
- Configuration and planning blueprints
- Cutover and hypercare support plan

## 2. CI-DS Integration Between SAP IBP and S/4HANA

- Design CI-DS integration between SAP IBP and S/4HANA for master and transactional data
- Support delta planning data exchange and monitoring
- Document integration architecture and handover to internal teams

Outputs:
- Integration design and mapping documentation
- Error-handling and monitoring guidelines
- Handover package for operations

## 3. SAP PP and S/4HANA Manufacturing Planning

- Configure SAP S/4HANA PP planning and logistics processes for manufacturing clients
- Conduct fit-gap analysis, solution design, and testing based on business requirements
- Support go-live cutover and hypercare for planning stabilisation

Outputs:
- Fit-gap and solution design documents
- Test scenarios and results
- Go-live and hypercare playbook

## 4. MOC/ECM Workflow Design for Compliance

- Design Management of Change and Engineering Change Management workflows
- Align SAP processes with audit and regulatory readiness (e.g. FDA, CAPA)
- Support stakeholders in adopting structured change processes

Outputs:
- Workflow and approval design
- RACI and stakeholder maps
- Compliance-focused reporting views

## 5. SAP Training and Enablement

- Deliver SAP PP / logistics and planning training for consultant and key-user cohorts
- Design training material, run train-the-trainer activities, and evaluate outcomes
- Adapt content for different levels (consultants, business users, IT teams)

Outputs:
- Training curriculum and materials
- Train-the-trainer packages
- Evaluation and improvement reports
